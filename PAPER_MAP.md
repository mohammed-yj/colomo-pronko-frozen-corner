# Paper–code map

Authority: `paper/colomo-pronko-conjecture.pdf` and the accompanying final supplement. Paper labels are retained in the TeX. “Paper proof” explicitly means the full step has no corresponding end-to-end Lean theorem. Exact Python ranges are documented in `REPRODUCIBILITY.md`; they do not prove arbitrary-parameter statements.
