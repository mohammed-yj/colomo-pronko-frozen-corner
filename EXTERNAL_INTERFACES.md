## Integral convention and version control

Use the **PDF numbering** of [CP24 v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.04358v1). The HTML conversion renumbers displays and is unsuitable for identifying (2.8)–(2.9). Set CP24's lattice size to `n`, its `r=n−s`, and its freezing parameter to `s`; the number of integrations is `d=n−2s`. Both polynomial orders become `r`. At the ice point the second argument is `eta(z)=z/(z−1)`. The prefactor simplifies to `A_r²/d!` using `b_j=A_(j−1)/A_j`. The paper specifies positive small circles around `1`, where the pair denominators are nonzero, before making coefficient/residue manipulations.

The other versioned sources are [CP v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14006v3), [FR v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24548v1), and [IOTZ v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0411280v2). Bibliographic details are in the preserved paper. Third-party PDFs are not redistributed.

For a logical reading of the final theorem, combine: external enumeration and integral inputs → paper Theorem 6.1; paper Proposition 4.1 → CP/FR candidate and `det K=A`; these facts supply the normalization records → Lean normalized finite bridges; paper endpoint arguments → Theorem 2.1. Corollary 2.2 then uses the separate external determinant limit. The repository does not claim that this whole chain has been formalized.
